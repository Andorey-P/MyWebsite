# About-Page Template

Fill-in skeleton for the about page. Order matters — credibility before personality. Full rationale in [../docs/about-page-strategy.md](../docs/about-page-strategy.md).

---

### 1. Role restatement
*One line, same thesis as the hero, different words — so the about page doesn't feel disconnected.*

> [Restated role/specialty in one sentence.]

### 2. Credibility block *(scannable — a reader should extract this in five seconds without reading the prose)*

- Years / specialization: [e.g. "6 years building WebGL experiences for design studios and DTC brands"]
- Notable clients/studios: [names, only real ones]
- Recognition (if genuine): [awards, features — omit entirely if none, don't pad]
- Core technical specialty claim: [e.g. "custom GLSL shader work, real-time procedural systems"]

*If early-career with no marquee names yet: substitute a specific ambitious personal project, competition placement, or depth-of-skill claim — see the early-career substitution guidance in [../docs/about-page-strategy.md](../docs/about-page-strategy.md#rule-proof-points-are-specific-facts-not-adjectives).*

### 3. The narrative paragraph *(150–300 words total for the whole page's running prose — this is most of that budget)*
*Needs one real turn: a specific realization, constraint, or pivot that explains why this specialty, why now. Not a chronology.*

> [What you did before this, if relevant, told as a specific detail not a job title list.] [The turn — the moment/realization/constraint that led to the current specialty.] [What that specialty actually means in practice today.]

### 4. The human angle *(one detail, specific enough to be falsifiable — not "I love coffee and hiking")*

> [A specific, real detail about how you spend time on your craft outside of client work, ideally one that quietly reinforces the technical thesis.]

### 5. CTA
*Matches [../snippets/cta-copy-patterns.md](../snippets/cta-copy-patterns.md) — restate availability/contact.*

> [CTA line.]

---

## Self-check before finalizing

- [ ] Does credibility come before personality, in that order?
- [ ] Is every proof point a checkable fact, not an adjective?
- [ ] Does the narrative paragraph have an actual turn, or is it a résumé timeline in prose form?
- [ ] Is the human-angle detail specific enough that it couldn't be copy-pasted onto a different portfolio unchanged?
- [ ] Total running prose in the 150–300 word range — not a wall of text?
- [ ] Voice consistent with the hero and case studies (same person talking)?
