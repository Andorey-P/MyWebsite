# Hero Messaging Template

Fill-in skeleton for hero copy. Messaging only — layout is `ui-ux-designer`'s call, entrance animation is `animation-principles`'s call. Full rationale in [../docs/hero-section-strategy.md](../docs/hero-section-strategy.md).

---

**Eyebrow / role label** *(2–4 words, the fastest-scannable identity claim)*
> [Role — e.g. "Creative Developer", "Interactive Designer & Developer"]

**Identity + specialty sentence** *(one sentence, concrete nouns, no decoding required, must survive alone without the 3D scene)*
> I [what you build] for [who it's for / what kind of work] — [the specific differentiator: a technique, a sensibility, an outcome].

**Optional second line** *(only if it earns its place — a sharper, more specific restatement or a craft signal; cut if the first sentence already does the job)*
> [Optional supporting line.]

**Availability / status line** *(optional but strong — doubles as trust signal and soft urgency)*
> Based in [location] · [Available for freelance / Open to new roles / Currently at Capacity — booking Q_]

**Primary CTA** *(points at proof, not at contact)*
> [View Work ↓ / See the Work / Selected Work]

**Secondary CTA** *(present, lower emphasis — for the visitor who already knows they want to talk)*
> [About / Get in Touch]

---

## Self-check before finalizing

- [ ] Does the identity + specialty sentence pass the 3-second skim test — one read, no rereading to find the subject?
- [ ] Does it survive as a screenshot / on a slow connection / with the 3D scene not yet loaded?
- [ ] Does it avoid opening with the tech stack?
- [ ] Is the primary CTA pointed at work, not contact?
- [ ] Would this sentence sound false pasted onto a different creative developer's site? (If not false — i.e. generic enough to fit anyone — rewrite for specificity.)
